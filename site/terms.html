Acceptable use

My Jarvis can read your screen, type, click and run commands. Do not point it at systems you are not authorised to operate, and do not use it to capture other people's data without their knowledge. You are responsible for what it does on your behalf.

Privacy

There is no My Jarvis server, no account and no telemetry. Configuration, memories and API keys live encrypted in ~/.jarvis/ on your own machine.

Your AI provider is the exception. Whatever you send My Jarvis — text, and screenshots when vision is on — is transmitted to the provider you configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA or Mistral) and handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy, not these. Run a local Ollama model if you want nothing to leave your machine.

Optional integrations reach the services you connect them to: Google Calendar, Todoist, Notion, your mail provider, Home Assistant, Philips Hue. Each is off until you set it up.
