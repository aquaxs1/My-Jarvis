4. Privacy

There is no My Jarvis server, no account and no telemetry. Configuration, memories and API keys live encrypted in ~/.jarvis/ on your own machine. On first start the app lists what it sends where and only starts sending after you accept that once.

Your AI provider receives what you send My Jarvis — text, the conversation so far, and screenshots when vision or Copilot is in use — and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA or Mistral). A local Ollama model keeps this on your machine.

Voice input goes to Google. Speech is turned into text by Google's speech recognition, also when you use a local model. With the wake word switched on and no Porcupine access key, everything said near the microphone is sent to Google in short snippets to catch "Hey Jarvis" — so only switch it on where nobody minds. Typed input uses no Google service.

Briefings fetch the weather for your location from wttr.in and headlines from tagesschau.de, ORF and Hacker News. Optional integrations reach the services you connect them to: Google Calendar, Todoist, Notion, your mail provider, Home Assistant, Philips Hue. Each is off until you set it up.
